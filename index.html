Our Story

Four decades of coastal fruit

Jess Jackson bottled his first Vintner's Reserve Chardonnay in 1982. Forty years on it is still made the same way: grapes from cool coastal vineyards, picked by hand, fermented in small lots, aged in French oak.

Today the family farms across Sonoma, Monterey, Santa Barbara and Mendocino, all certified sustainable.
